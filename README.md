# MIGRAGENT

You are applying to move country, or to get licensed to work in one. MIGRAGENT asks about your
situation once, reads the official sources itself, and gives you a guide you can save as a PDF.

It is not a chatbot. You fill in a form, you upload what you already have, and an agent goes away
and does the work.

**Nothing is stated without a source.** Every requirement in the guide carries the official page it
came from and the date it was read. Where no source can be found, the requirement is not stated; it
goes into open questions at the back.

Three lanes are covered properly: a Canada study permit, nursing registration in Canada, and UK and
EU skilled work visas.

See `docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md` for the whole thing in plain words, and `docs/PLAN.md` for what is built
and what is not.
