Three marks, judged at the size that matters

A. The gate An M whose centre stops short, so the space beneath is a doorway. The dot standing in it is the footnote marker. Says both halves of the product in one shape.
B. The stamp An entry stamp, landed slightly crooked. Warmest and most instantly readable. Also the most crowded space, because every visa brand already owns a stamp.
C. The footnote A claim and the raised marker that proves it. The most honest of the three and the least obvious. Says nothing at all about travel, which may be the point.
